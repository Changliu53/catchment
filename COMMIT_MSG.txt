Analysis primitives, validation, and the Harris County data pipeline

The model never emits executable code. It fills a closed schema of six
primitives, so its output needs no sanitisation: there is no escape hatch.
Correctness lives in a pure executor, tested independently of any model.

Pipeline notes worth keeping:
- Two geometries per block group. Full precision for measurement, 10 m
  simplification for display. Simplification moves boundaries by up to ~5% of
  area on the smallest polygons, so measuring against display geometry would
  be wrong; the schema makes that mistake impossible to express.
- All measurement happens in EPSG:32615, never in degrees. Recomputed county
  area is 4,606 km2 against an official 4,602.
- The Overpass query matched both Harris County, Texas and Harris County,
  Georgia. POIs are filtered by containment in the real county boundary
  rather than a bounding box.
- ACS -666666666 means suppressed, not zero, and is kept NULL. 250001 is a
  top code, not a real income, and is flagged so it is never averaged.
- flood_pct is the FEMA SFHA share; flood_pct_500 is tracked separately
  because many Houston homes that flooded in Harvey sat outside the SFHA.
